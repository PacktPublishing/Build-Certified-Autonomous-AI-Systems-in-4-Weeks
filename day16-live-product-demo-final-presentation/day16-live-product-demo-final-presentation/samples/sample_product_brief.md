# Sample Product Brief

## Product Name
StudySprint AI

## One-Line Idea
A simple AI study coach that turns a learner's topic into a short study plan, quiz, and improvement checklist.

## Target User
Busy beginners who want a focused way to study one topic at a time.

## Problem
Learners often do not know where to start, what to practice, or how to judge whether they understood the topic.

## Core Features Built
- Topic input
- Study plan generator
- Quiz generator
- Feedback and next-step suggestions
- Markdown export

## Demo Flow
1. Open the app.
2. Enter a topic like Python functions.
3. Generate a study plan.
4. Generate quiz questions.
5. Review the improvement checklist.
6. Export the final study guide.

## Strongest Demo Moment
The app turns one small topic into a complete learning package in seconds.

## Known Weak Spots
The first version does not track long-term progress yet, and the quiz difficulty is basic.

## Launch Goal
Share the app with early learners, collect feedback, and decide what to improve in the next version.
