# Product Brief: LessonBlueprint

## 1. Product Summary

A web-based platform to help course creators create and organize lab plans using a visual, feature-rich interface.

## 2. Target User

Busy course creators who need to turn rough lesson ideas into polished lab plans for their students.

## 3. Painful Workflow

The pain point of having messy notes, scattered feature ideas, and no clear structure for what the student should build in the lab.

## 4. Desired Output

A clear product brief with a target user, painful workflow, features, score, and capstone-ready plan.

## 5. Why This Could Spread

Someone would share or recommend this to others due to its potential impact on course creation efficiency and student outcomes.

## 6. Prototype Features

- Visual lab plan structure
- Feature-rich interface for easy feature organization
- Collaboration tools

## 7. Capstone Fit

This is realistic for the final capstone as it addresses a common pain point in course creation and provides a clear, structured solution.
