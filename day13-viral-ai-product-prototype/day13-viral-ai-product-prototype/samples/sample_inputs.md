# Day 13 Sample Inputs

Use these examples during the demo to show how the product idea generator turns messy input into a clear capstone direction.

## Example 1 — Course Creator

**Target user**

```text
Busy course creators who need to turn rough lesson ideas into polished lab plans
```

**Painful workflow**

```text
They have messy notes, scattered feature ideas, and no clear structure for what the student should build in the lab.
```

**Desired output**

```text
A clear product brief with product name, target user, painful workflow, features, score, and capstone-ready plan.
```

## Example 2 — Small Business Owner

**Target user**

```text
Small business owners who get many customer messages from email, Instagram, and WhatsApp
```

**Painful workflow**

```text
They spend too much time reading messages, figuring out what is urgent, and writing the same kind of replies again and again.
```

**Desired output**

```text
A prioritized response plan with suggested replies and follow-up tasks.
```

## Example 3 — Job Seeker

**Target user**

```text
Job seekers applying to many roles every week
```

**Painful workflow**

```text
They copy and paste job descriptions, compare them against their resume manually, and struggle to know what to improve before applying.
```

**Desired output**

```text
A resume improvement brief with missing keywords, stronger bullet points, and application readiness score.
```
