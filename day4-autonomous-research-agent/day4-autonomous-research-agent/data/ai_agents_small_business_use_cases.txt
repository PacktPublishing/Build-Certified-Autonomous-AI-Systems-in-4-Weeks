Title: Use Cases for AI Agents in Small Businesses

AI agents are useful in small businesses when they can follow a goal, choose a tool, take an action, observe the result, and continue until the task is complete.

A sales agent can summarize leads, draft follow-up emails, and remind the owner which customers need attention.

A support agent can answer common questions, search company documents, create support tickets, and escalate complex issues to a human.

A finance agent can categorize expenses, explain invoice status, prepare payment reminders, and help summarize cash-flow information.

A marketing agent can generate blog outlines, social media drafts, campaign ideas, and customer personas based on business notes.

A research agent can collect information about competitors, summarize market trends, and prepare a short report for decision makers.

The safest approach is to keep agents narrow, connect them only to approved tools, and require human review before sending messages, spending money, or changing important records.
